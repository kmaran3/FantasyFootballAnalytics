Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Email address — used for account identification and login
  • Display name — shown on your profile

When you use the app, we store:

  • Saved rankings — custom player rankings you create
  • Draft sessions — mock draft and live draft board data
  • League connections — Sleeper or ESPN league IDs you connect (we do not store your league credentials)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Save and display your custom rankings and draft data
  • Connect to third-party fantasy platforms (Sleeper, ESPN) on your behalf

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Google OAuth — optional sign-in method
  • Sleeper API — league data retrieval (only when you connect a league)
  • ESPN API — league data retrieval (only when you connect a league)

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. We use CSRF protection, encrypted sessions, and HTTPS to protect your data in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your saved rankings and draft data at any time through the app. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.

6. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

Darkhorse Fantasy Sports is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@darkhorsefantasysports.com.
